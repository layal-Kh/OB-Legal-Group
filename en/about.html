About us

Learn about OB Legal Group and its approach to supporting companies and investors with legal and commercial matters.

A partner in navigating legal and business challenges

OB Legal Group supports companies, investors and individuals with legal and commercial matters that may arise when establishing, managing and expanding a business or entering different markets. We begin by understanding client goals, business context and relevant risks, then define an appropriate scope of support based on the country concerned.

We currently work in Turkey, the UAE and Syria, with comprehensive coverage of Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Egypt, and partial coverage of other Arab countries and the European Union. Consultations are provided online, and partner local lawyers handle court representation under our supervision.

Our vision and mission

Our vision is to be a trusted resource helping clients understand legal and commercial matters clearly and professionally. Our mission is to provide considered, practical support that helps organize transactions, navigate challenges and make informed decisions.

Our values

  • Clarity on scope and expected next steps
  • Professionalism and attention to detail
  • Understanding client needs and commercial goals
  • Respect for information privacy
  • Collaboration with partner local lawyers where needed

Legal and business services with an international perspective, supporting companies and investors with transactions and challenges.
