Cases & dispute resolution

Legal support to assess disputes and manage resolution options, from negotiation and settlement to arbitration and the supervision of local lawyers, depending on the matter and the country concerned.

Legal services

  • Civil and commercial disputes arising from rights and obligations.
  • Legal claims relating to breach of contract or non-performance of obligations.
  • Commercial arbitration: representing parties, acting as arbitrator, and reviewing arbitration clauses.
  • Mediation and negotiation to reach agreed solutions.
  • Settling disputes and drafting settlement agreements.
  • Contractual disputes over interpretation, performance or termination.
  • Cross-border issues relating to jurisdiction and foreign judgments.
  • Legal supervision of criminal, labor and family cases.

How can we help?

  • Reviewing the facts and documents and identifying the main points of disagreement.
  • Assessing the available paths and the possible risks of each.
  • Helping prepare claims and legal correspondence.
  • Supporting negotiation and evaluating settlement proposals.
  • Supervising partner local lawyers who handle representation before the courts.

Who can benefit?

Companies, individuals, investors, business partners, and parties to local or cross-border disputes.

Scope of service

We do not appear before courts ourselves; partner local lawyers handle court representation under our supervision. In arbitration, we act as arbitrators and also represent the parties.

Where we work and what we cover

  • We currently work in: Türkiye, the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, and Syria.
  • Comprehensive coverage: The United Kingdom, Saudi Arabia, and Egypt.
  • Partial coverage: other Arab countries and the European Union.
